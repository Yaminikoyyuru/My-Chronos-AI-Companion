    Why this exists

    Many older people spend long hours with nobody to talk to, and their words are often brushed aside. They also fall, and a fall that nobody notices can turn serious. ChronosAI Companion tries to help with both: it talks, quizzes, plays songs, reads news and reminds about meals and medicines, and it quietly watches for trouble.

    The World Health Organization estimates about 684 000 deaths from falls each year, second only to road traffic among unintentional injury deaths. Adults over 60 suffer the most fatal falls, and about 37.3 million falls a year need medical attention (WHO, Falls fact sheet, accessed October 2026).

    How it works

    See
    OpenCV 5 finds the face (YuNet) and measures motion with optical flow. Three situations raise a flag: a possible fall, staying unusually still, or leaving the frame.
    Ask
    The robot speaks first: “Are you okay?” The question, the waiting time and whether movement counts as an answer depend on what the camera saw. If there is no reply it asks once more.
    Act
    If the person cannot confirm they are safe, the agent saves one snapshot to Amazon S3, builds a review page and emails the physician through Amazon SES.
    Decide
    The physician opens the page and chooses Dispatch or False alarm. The robot reads the decision back from S3 and tells the person what is happening. The robot never dispatches help on its own.

    The OpenCV pipeline runs on the local computer. Each decision is also written to agent_trace.jsonl.

    Respect for privacy

    • No continuous recording. One snapshot is stored only when an alert is escalated.
    • Say “I need a break” or “good night” to pause monitoring. Pauses end by themselves, and “help” always overrides a pause.
    • Stored objects and review links expire automatically.

    What it cannot do yet

    • Fall detection is a heuristic, not a trained model, and the test set is small (one person).
    • Voice uses Google speech recognition, needs internet and understands English only.
    • On our AWS account DynamoDB, Lambda, SNS and Bedrock were not available, so incident storage uses S3 and risk is rule-based.
    • It is a research prototype, not a medical device, and does not replace emergency services.

    What is next

    A trained pose model for falls, voice in Telugu and Hindi, and an evaluation with more people. The same see, ask, escalate loop could also watch a driver who leaves the seat or stops responding, with consent and separate in-vehicle testing.
